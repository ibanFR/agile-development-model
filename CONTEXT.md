# Context

Glossary for this repository. Terms only — no implementation detail.

## Model

**Software Development Model** — the subject of this repository: the agile approach to
product feature delivery that the whole workspace describes. Modelled as a single software
system.

**Focus area** — one of the five parts the model is divided into: Align & Understand, BDD,
DDD, XP, and Lean Product Development. A focus area groups the components that belong to it.

**Component** — a single practice, artefact or ceremony within a focus area, such as a
Product Brief, Pair Programming or a Product Backlog. Components are what a team actually
does; focus areas are how those practices are grouped.

## Documentation

**View** — one named diagram of the model, at a chosen level of detail: the system context,
the focus areas, the components of one focus area, or the components of all of them.

**Local viewer** — the local authoring surface, used to write and check the model while
editing it. What it shows is not published, and it lays views out independently of the
generated site.

**Generated site** — the published documentation, built from the model. This is the artefact
readers see, and it lays views out independently of the local viewer.

**ADR** — a record of a decision that is hard to reverse, surprising without its reasoning,
and the result of a real trade-off.
